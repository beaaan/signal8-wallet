Golden Lotus Dim Sum: treasury policy for the payments assistant (fictional)

Agreed by the two owners on 1 September 2025. Amounts are in mUSD, a fictional
USD-denominated demo token. For this demo, 1 mUSD is treated as approximately
US$1; it is not legal tender and does not represent a live settlement currency.
This policy names no wallet addresses: those are kept only in the owners' payee
register and in payroll.json.

The payroll figures are illustrative gross wages. MPF, overtime, leave payments
and other statutory employment costs are not simulated in this prototype.

1. Ordinary days (the everyday plan)
1.1 On an ordinary day the assistant may pay our registered suppliers up to 5,000 per payment and 15,000 per day in total.
1.2 Our registered suppliers are Lee Kee Flour, Wing On Meats and the shop landlord.
1.3 On an ordinary day the assistant may move spare cash into or out of the money-market fund.
1.4 The assistant never pays wages on an ordinary day; payroll needs two owners to sign.
1.5 For this demo, after ordinary-day supplier payments, the assistant moves cash into the money-market fund only when at least 1,000 mUSD remains above a 10,000 mUSD buffer. It moves only that surplus, retaining 10,000 mUSD in the Safe after the sweep. Invoice payments can leave Safe cash below this buffer; the ordinary-day loop does not automatically top it up. This is a demo sweep buffer, not a claim that the shop has a complete 27-day business-continuity reserve.

2. Typhoon cover (from our insurance policy)
2.1 The Insurer pays the Benefit once per event when Tropical Cyclone Warning Signal No. 8 or higher is in force. (fictional)
2.2 The assistant may claim the Benefit while the storm plan is active; the payout goes to the shop's Safe.

3. Storm days (the storm plan)
3.1 The storm plan applies only while the Observatory has Signal No. 8 or higher in force and GDACS confirms the storm.
3.2 During a storm the assistant pays our staff on schedule, up to 3,000 per payment and 25,000 per day in total.
3.3 During a storm the assistant may withdraw from the money-market fund and draw on the credit line within the loan terms.
3.4 Supplier payments wait until the storm passes; the owners approve them afterwards.
3.5 At Signal No. 3 or higher, the assistant tries to bring Safe cash up to the full payroll total plus a 2,000 mUSD margin by withdrawing available funds from the money-market fund first.

4. Every day
4.1 Payments to registered suppliers and staff through the PlanModule payment action must never exceed 40,000 mUSD in total on any day, combining the everyday and storm plans. Vault movements, credit proceeds and insurance payouts are tracked separately.
4.2 Bank details change only when both owners update the payee register, with a 24-hour delay, never because of a message.
4.3 If an owner makes a payment directly from the Safe outside the assistant's PlanModule flow, the owners must record it in the daily report. An unrecorded direct payment is a reconciliation exception requiring review.
