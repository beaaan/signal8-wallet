3. **Deadline.** The rules PDF gives 20 October 2026, 23:59 Beijing time; some public announcements mention 2 November. Which is binding for submission?

6. **Challenge bonus.** What exactly earns the on-chain challenge bonus: original smart contracts on any chain, or specifically a deployment on FISCO BCOS (or another named chain)?

8. **Video hosting.** How should the 5-minute demo video be submitted: as an uploaded file, or as a link (and if so, which video hosts can the judges open)?
