# Finance track contribution

Contributed by Ngọc, Dung and Hân. Imported on 14 October 2026.

## This version

This is the original contribution with Vietnamese prose translated into English.
Names, figures, assumptions, recommendations and checklist states are preserved.
The live Google Sheet URL is intentionally omitted for privacy; the committed
XLSX and CSV exports supply the workbook content. Other research links remain.
The workbook was already in English and is copied byte for byte.
The import does not endorse or correct the contribution's claims. Research,
workbook corrections and alignment with the implemented demo follow in separate
pull requests. This is not a final deck, a timed rehearsal or a product approval.

## Files

- [Finance pack](finance-pack.md): product research, forecast discussion,
  business case, paperwork notes, pitch script, Q&A and deck outline.
- [Cash-flow workbook](ragasa-cashflow.xlsx): the original five sheets,
  formulas and chart.
- `exports/*.csv`: values exported from this exact workbook, one file per sheet.
  They do not preserve formulas, formatting or the chart. Do not edit them by
  hand. Update the workbook and regenerate its exports together.
